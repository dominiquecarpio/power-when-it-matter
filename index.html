How much does a generator cost?
Whole-home standby generator systems typically range from $6,000 to $20,000+ installed, depending on size, fuel type, transfer switch, and site work. Commercial systems vary widely. We provide free written estimates.
How long does installation take?
Most residential installations are completed in 1–2 days. Commercial installations may take several days depending on the scope of work, permitting timelines, and site conditions.
What size generator do I need?
Sizing depends on your home or business's square footage, the appliances you want to run, and your fuel type. Our technicians perform a load analysis to recommend the correct size — typically 10–22 kW for homes and larger for commercial.
Do generators require maintenance?
Yes. Like any engine-powered equipment, standby generators require regular oil changes, filter replacements, battery testing, and load testing to remain reliable. Annual service is the minimum recommendation.
How often should service be performed?
We recommend servicing your generator at least once a year, ideally before hurricane season. Our Preventive Maintenance plan includes two visits per year for optimal reliability.
Do you offer emergency repairs?
Yes. We offer 24/7 emergency repair service throughout Tampa Bay and Greater Pinellas County. Call (813) 555-1234 any time for an immediate response.
What brands do you service?
We service all major brands including Generac, Kohler, Cummins, Briggs & Stratton, Champion, and most other manufacturers. Our technicians are factory-trained on leading generator lines.
Can you service commercial generators?
Absolutely. We handle commercial installations and maintenance for healthcare facilities, retail centers, office buildings, industrial sites, and property management portfolios throughout the Tampa Bay area.
